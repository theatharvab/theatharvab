02 / ABOUT

Curious by nature.
Evidence-led by design.

I'm a student at Cypress Woods High School interested in the physics behind high-performance engineering: heat transfer in rocket engines, high-speed aerodynamics, and the simulations that help engineers make better design decisions.

My first major independent research project combined a physics-based dataset, a PyTorch surrogate model, and OpenFOAM simulations to investigate regenerative cooling channel designs. I presented the work at the 2026 Texas Junior Academy of Science.

I try to document not only what worked, but also assumptions, limitations, and what needs to be tested next.
