02 / ABOUT

Learn the tool.
Test it on a real problem.

I'm a junior at Cypress Woods High School interested in propulsion, thermal-fluids, computational modeling, and motorsport. I like learning a tool by applying it to a problem instead of stopping at tutorials.

For my first independent research project, I built a physics-based dataset, trained a PyTorch surrogate, and compared a selected cooling-channel design against OpenFOAM CFD. I presented the work at TJAS 2026, where it received Honorable Mention.

The simulations are not experimental validation, and the model has limitations. I want the portfolio to make those boundaries clear, because that is part of the engineering.
